## FEA Suite Overview
Abaqus, part of the SIMULIA portfolio, is a benchmark tool for finite element analysis across industries where physical accuracy is critical. It discretizes a geometry into a mesh of elements and solves the governing equations of mechanics to reveal stress, strain, displacement, and temperature under real-world loads.

The suite is known for its strength in nonlinear problems. Where linear tools struggle, Abaqus handles material plasticity, hyperelasticity, complex contact between parts, and geometric nonlinearity with a robust implicit solver and a complementary explicit solver for high-speed dynamic events like impacts and crashes.

Beyond structures, Abaqus supports coupled physics, thermal-stress, fluid-structure interaction, and electromagnetics, enabling multiphysics studies within one environment. Its scripting interface exposes the full model through Python, letting teams automate parametric studies and integrate simulation into larger engineering workflows.

## Getting Started Playbook
Launch Abaqus/CAE, create a part by importing CAD geometry or building it natively, and assign material properties and section definitions. Mesh the model with an appropriate element type, then define the loads, boundary conditions, and interactions that represent your scenario.

Choose the implicit or explicit solver based on the problem's time scale, submit the job, and monitor its progress. When the analysis completes, open the visualization module to inspect contour plots, animate deformation, and extract quantitative results for reporting.
